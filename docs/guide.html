Vouches, prices and tickets for one trader.

Donut Vouch Bot runs a DonutSMP trading server. It posts vouches with proof, keeps live Skeleton spawner prices, opens private tickets and shares build schematics.

What the bot does

  • Vouches. The owner posts spawner, build and giveaway vouches. Each is one card with a vouch picture and an optional proof picture, and the bot numbers them.
  • Prices. A live price panel shows what Skeleton spawners cost to buy and what they pay when sold. Two calculator commands do the maths for anyone.
  • Tickets. Customers open a private ticket to buy, sell or ask for help. The owner claims and closes it.
  • Schematics. Builds are shared in dedicated channels with a build number, size and creator credit. Booster channels show pictures only.
  • Summary. One panel shows the totals and recent trades, and refreshes itself after every vouch.

The bot is built for a single trader, so everything is written from one person's point of view: "I sell to you" and "bought from me". Only Skeleton spawners are traded.

Who can use what

LevelWhoWhat they can do
EveryoneEvery server memberUse /spawnerbuy and /spawnersell, press the price panel buttons, and chat in their own ticket.
OwnerThe owner, plus anyone with the one optional command roleEvery other command and every ticket control. The role works exactly like the owner.

There are no other staff roles. Manage Server, Manage Channels and being the ticket opener give no extra access.

For customers

You do not need any setup. Everything you can do is in the server.

Check a price

Find the price panel. I SELL TO YOU is what you pay to buy a spawner. YOU SELL TO ME is what you get when you sell one. To work out a total, use the calculators:

  • /spawnerbuy amount:128 shows what 128 spawners cost you.
  • /spawnersell amount:128 shows what you earn selling 128.

Both show the amount (with stacks), the price each and the total, using the live prices on the panel.

Open a ticket

Press Buy Spawners or Sell Spawners on the price panel, fill in your in-game name and the amount, and a private channel opens with the owner. For anything else, use the General Support panel. You can have one open ticket of each type at a time. The owner closes the ticket when you are done.

Reading a vouch

Each vouch in the vouch channels is one card. It shows the type of trade, its value, a picture of the vouch and, usually, a proof picture. The counter line (for example Build - 12 • Total - 140) is how many vouches of that type there are, and how many in all.

Amounts and prices

Anywhere a command asks for a price or value, you can type it in short form. The bot shows it back in both forms.

You typeThe bot shows
16.2m(16.2m) 16,200,000
3.44b(3.44b) 3,440,000,000
250k(250k) 250,000
$3,0003,000
1t(1t) 1,000,000,000,000

Units are k (thousand), m (million), b (billion) and t (trillion). Spawner amounts also show stacks of 64, for example 105 is 1 stack + 41.

Purge options

/purge never deletes anything until you confirm. It shows the exact count and the filters first, and gives you 30 seconds to press Delete or Cancel.

OptionWhat it does
amountHow many messages to delete, 1 to 100.
userOnly that person's messages.
filterEverything, humans only, bots only, messages with attachments, with links or with embeds.
containsOnly messages containing this text.
scanHow many recent messages to look through, up to 500 (default 100). Raise it when your filters match older messages.
include_pinnedAlso delete pinned messages. They are skipped by default.

Discord cannot bulk delete messages older than 14 days, so those are skipped. The contains, links, attachments and embeds filters also need the Message Content Intent (see Setup).

Vouches

A vouch is public proof of a finished trade. The owner posts one with /spawnervouch, /buildvouch or /giveawayvouch. Every vouch is one card, posted to the all-vouches channel and copied to its own channel.

What is on a card

  • A title with the type: 💀 Spawner Vouch, 🏗️ Build Vouch or 🎁 Giveaway Vouch.
  • A counter line. In the all-vouches channel it reads Build - 12 • Total - 140. In the spawner, build and giveaway channels it shows only the category number, like Build - 12.
  • The details: the trade (Customer bought from me or Customer sold to me), the value, and the amount for spawners.
  • Proof of vouch, which is the vouch picture. It is always there.
  • A proof picture, which is optional. It is labelled Proof of trade on spawners, Proof of base on builds and Proof of payment on giveaways.
  • On builds, an optional .litematic file as a download.

Where vouches go

VouchPosted to
SpawnerAll-vouches channel, and the spawner channel if one is set
BuildAll-vouches channel, and the build channel if one is set
GiveawayAll-vouches channel, and the giveaway channel if one is set

How numbering works

Each vouch has two numbers: its place among vouches of its type, and its place among all vouches (the Total). Only the all-vouches channel shows the Total. New vouches take the next free number, and vouches are posted one at a time, so two quick vouches never share a number.

Removing a vouch

Posted something by mistake? Do not delete the post by hand. The bot still keeps its record and its number, which leaves a gap. Use /vouchremove instead.

  1. Look at the Total number on the vouch in the all-vouches channel, for example 31.
  2. Run /vouchremove number:31. The bot shows the vouch and how many later vouches will move, and asks you to confirm.
  3. Press Remove. The bot deletes the vouch everywhere, moves every later vouch down by one (the Total and its type number) and edits those posts. The summary panel updates too.

Removing an early vouch edits many posts, and Discord limits how fast messages can be edited, so it can take a while. The bot tells you when it is done. If it lists posts it could not edit, fix the cause it shows and run /vouchfix. It re-checks every post and skips the ones that are already right.

The summary panel

/summary posts one panel with an overview (total vouches and total traded), then spawners, builds and giveaways, each with recent trades. It refreshes after every vouch, when the bot starts and every 10 minutes. Posting a new one replaces the old one.

Tickets

A ticket is a private channel between one customer and the owner.

  • Spawner tickets open from the price panel. A form asks for the in-game name and the amount. The ticket then shows the name, whether it is a buy or a sell, the amount, the current prices and the total at today's price.
  • Support tickets open from the General Support panel (posted with /ticketpanel). One button, no form.
  • When a ticket opens, the customer, the owner and the command role (if set) are pinged.
  • Each person can have one open ticket per type.

Closing and managing

Only the owner (and the command role) can close, claim, unclaim or add people. Each action is a button in the ticket and a command, so you never have to scroll up to the first message.

Schematics

Schematic channels share builds. You drop in the pictures and the file, and the bot re-posts them in one tidy post with a build number, the size and a credit. It then removes your message.

Two kinds of channel

KindAdd it withWhat the bot postsNumbers
Posting channel/schematicchannel addThe pictures and the schematic fileB1, B2...
Booster channel/boosterchannel addThe pictures only. The file is never re-posted, and the booster notice is addedBB1, BB2...

Both commands take a channel and a prefix of 1 to 3 letters. The letters start the build number, and each prefix counts up on its own. A channel can only be one kind. Use list and remove to review or change them.

Posting a schematic

  1. In a schematic channel, send one message with the pictures and the schematic file. Type the build name on the first line. If you leave it out, the file name is used.
  2. The bot posts it right away and deletes your message. It credits the owner. To credit someone else, @mention them in your message.
  3. Need to fix the name? Send the new name as a normal message within 90 seconds, or reply to the bot's post with it at any time. Mentions in that message change the creator.

In a booster channel the file is optional. If you attach it, it is only read to work out the size.

  • Forwarded messages work. Use Discord's Forward button to send a message with pictures and a file into the channel. The bot reads them from the forwarded copy. A forward cannot carry text, so the name is the schematic's file name.
  • If the files are missing, the bot tells you for 15 seconds and posts nothing.

What the post looks like

⚠ DO NOT SHARE THESE SCHEMATICS ⚠
#  🔨 Mega Castle 🔨
🍩 Tomberzz's Schematics

BUILD NUMBER: B7
Size = 120 x 64 x 97 = 744,960
Taking this schematic and reposting it in your own server without
credit or permission will result in removal of access...
 Original Creator: @owner

The size is read from a .litematic file: width x height x length, then the total (the three multiplied). If the file cannot be read, the size says Unknown.

Editing a schematic later

Run /schematicedit build:B7 with any of name, creator, number (for example B12) and size. The bot edits its own post and keeps the pictures and file. A number that is already used is refused, and new posts never reuse a number you set by hand. Only posts made since this feature was added can be edited.

Requires the Message Content Intent. The bot cannot read your message otherwise. Turn it on as described in Setup, set MESSAGE_CONTENT_INTENT=true, and give the bot Send Messages, Attach Files and Manage Messages in the schematic channels.

Removing a schematic

Run /schematicremove build:B3 and confirm. The bot deletes the post, then moves every later build with the same prefix down by one (B4 becomes B3) and edits those posts. Prefixes count separately, so removing B3 never changes BB numbers.

The bot saves only a tiny text record per post (message, name, creator and size, about 80 bytes). Pictures and files stay in Discord and are never stored by the bot.

Setup

This part is for whoever runs the bot. You need Node 18.17 or newer, a Discord account that can manage the server, and a few minutes.

Create the bot in the Developer Portal

  1. Go to discord.com/developers/applications, choose New Application and name it. On the General Information page, copy the Application ID.
  2. Open the Bot tab, choose Reset Token and copy it. This is DISCORD_TOKEN. Treat it like a password: never post it, and reset it if it leaks.
  3. Still on the Bot tab, under Privileged Gateway Intents, leave Presence and Server Members off. Turn Message Content Intent on only if you want schematic posting or the text filters of /purge.
  4. Invite the bot with the link below, and pick your server.
  5. In Discord, turn on Developer Mode (User Settings, Advanced). Right-click your server, yourself and each channel, and choose Copy ID for the settings below.
  6. Make sure the bot can see and post in the vouch channels and the ticket category, and has Manage Channels on that category.

Invite link

https://discord.com/oauth2/authorize?client_id=YOUR_APP_ID&permissions=257040&scope=bot%20applications.commands

Permissions and intents

The invite link asks for permission number 257040. The bot does not need Administrator or Manage Roles.

PermissionWhy the bot needs it
View Channel, Send Messages, Embed LinksPanels, calculators, vouches and tickets
Attach FilesRe-uploads the vouch, proof and schematic files
Read Message HistoryPurge, reading tickets, and editing its own posts
Manage MessagesPurge, and removing your message after a schematic post
Manage ChannelsCreating, locking and deleting ticket channels
Mention EveryonePinging the command role in new tickets. Skip it if that role allows anyone to mention it

The only intent in use by default is Guilds, which needs no setting. If you set MESSAGE_CONTENT_INTENT=true without switching the intent on in the Developer Portal, the bot cannot log in.

Settings in the .env file

Copy .env.example to .env and fill it in. Empty optional settings are skipped.

ALL_VOUCHES_CHANNEL_IDYesEvery vouch is posted here.
GUILD_IDNoRegisters commands in this server so they appear at once. Empty means global, which can take up to an hour.

SPAWNER_VOUCHES_CHANNEL_IDNoSpawner vouches are also copied here.
BUILD_VOUCHES_CHANNEL_IDNoBuild vouches are also copied here.

MESSAGE_CONTENT_INTENTNoSet to true for schematic posting and the purge text filters.
SCHEMATIC_BRAND, SCHEMATIC_BOOSTER_BRANDNoThe brand line on schematic and booster posts.
SCHEMATIC_EMOJI_WARN, _BUILD, _BRANDNoReplace the three emojis in schematic posts. Use the form <:name:id>, from a server the bot is in. The bot also needs Use External Emojis where it posts.
SCHEMATIC_CHANNELS, SCHEMATIC_BOOSTER_CHANNELSNoOptional. Channels can be added with the commands instead, so you can leave these empty.

Then run npm install and npm start. The slash commands register by themselves when the bot starts.

Dependencies are discord.js 14.19 or newer and dotenv. npm install adds both.

Hosting

On Wispbyte

Wispbyte pulls the code from GitHub each time the server starts, so to update the bot you push, then restart.

The .env file is not in GitHub, so create it in Wispbyte's file manager next to package.json. Restart the server after you change it, because it is only read at startup.

Keep the data/ folder. It holds the vouches, prices, panels and ticket claims. It is not in GitHub on purpose, so updates never overwrite it.

Anywhere else

The bot logs errors instead of crashing, but a host can still stop it. To restart it automatically, use pm2:

npm i -g pm2
pm2 start src/index.js --name vouch-bot
pm2 save

Troubleshooting

Commands do not show up in Discord

Re-invite the bot with the applications.commands scope, and restart it. Without GUILD_ID, new commands can take up to an hour. With it, they appear on restart. Reload Discord (Ctrl+R) if they still do not show.

The bot says "Only the owner can use this command"

Only the user in VOUCH_TARGET_ID and anyone with COMMAND_ROLE_ID can use owner commands. Check that the ID is yours and that the role ID is the role, not the server.

Nothing happens when I post in a schematic channel
  • Set MESSAGE_CONTENT_INTENT=true, switch the intent on in the Developer Portal and restart.
  • Run /schematicchannel list or /boosterchannel list to confirm the channel was added.
  • Post as the owner or someone with the command role, with the files in the same message.
  • Give the bot View Channel, Send Messages, Attach Files and Manage Messages there.
The bot fails to log in

The token is wrong or was reset (Bot tab, then Reset Token), or MESSAGE_CONTENT_INTENT=true is set while the intent is off in the Developer Portal.

A vouch number was skipped

A vouch post was probably deleted by hand, so the bot still counts it. Run /vouchremove with that Total number. It works even if the post is already gone, and it moves the later vouches down.

/vouchremove says it could not edit a post

The message under each post shows the reason. "Missing Access" or "Missing Permissions" means the bot needs View Channel and Read Message History in that channel. "Unknown Message" means the post is gone. After you fix it, run /vouchfix.

The price panel will not post

The bot needs View Channel, Send Messages and Embed Links in that channel.

A schematic emoji shows as text

The bot must be in the server that owns the emoji, and needs Use External Emojis in the channel. You can swap the emoji with the SCHEMATIC_EMOJI_... settings.

"Node is too old"

Install Node 18.17 or newer.
